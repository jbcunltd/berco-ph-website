003 — How BERCO Works

Four conversations between you and the work.

Every BERCO interior begins with listening. From the first sketch to the final hinge, the process is unhurried — and the same designer follows your home through.

i.

Consult

A studio visit, or a house call. We discuss how you live before we discuss what you'll build.

ii.

Design

Hand drawings, then 3D — refined over two to three rounds, with materials laid out on the table.

iii.

Build

Fabricated in our Laguna atelier with European hardware and Philippine hardwoods, where appropriate.

iv.

Install

A small, dedicated team. Dust-controlled installation. A ten-year structural warranty signed at handover.

004 — A Note on Materials

Honest oak. Honed stone. A bronze pull that warms with time. We choose finishes that age in the right direction.

04 / Process & Materials — Desktop bercoph.com / process
Process

From first conversation
to final hinge.

A typical BERCO commission takes between four and six months. What follows is the path most of our houses walk — slowly, with one designer beside you the whole way.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A studio meeting. Budget and brief.

  2. 02

    Site Visit

    Measurements, light, the way you actually move.

  3. 03

    3D Design

    Two to three rounds of refinement, drawn in-house.

  4. 04

    Materials

    Samples on the table. Decisions made in daylight.

  5. 05

    Fabrication

    Built in our Laguna atelier, six to ten weeks.

  6. 06

    Installation

    A small, dedicated team. Dust-controlled.

  7. 07

    Warranty

    Ten-year structural. Lifetime studio relationship.

Materials & Hardware

The cabinet, then the cabinetry.

We work with a curated set of European fittings and Asian-sourced timbers. Below — a working list, kept short on purpose.

01 — Cabinetry Carcass

Marine-Grade Plywood.

CARB P2-certified, edge-banded with PUR adhesive. Tropic-stable for Philippine humidity.

02 — Door & Surface Finishes

Oak · Walnut · Lacquer · Stone.

Solid hardwoods, fenix-laminate, ten-coat matte lacquer, honed quartzite and travertine.

03 — Hardware

Blum · Hettich · Salice.

German and Italian-engineered hinges, drawer systems, soft-close and push-to-open mechanisms.

Concept Notes

A website that behaves like a printed monograph — slow, generous with margin, photography-led.

Typography. Playfair Display for editorial moments; Inter at 300–500 for body and UI. Generous line-height. No all-caps body copy.

Palette. Warm whites, stone, ink, with bronze and forest as restrained accents. No high-saturation marketing colours.

CTAs. Always consultative — never "buy", never "shop", never "free quote".

Mobile. Sticky Book + Call bar. Hamburger opens an editorial menu, not a list.
